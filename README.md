# Follow the Money

An interactive explorer from my **Payments, Decoded** series on Medium.

Pick a card (credit, debit, prepaid payroll, corporate or virtual) and step through a $50 purchase: tap, clearing, settlement and funding. At every step it shows which account changes, and answers one question: *where is the $50 right now?*

**Try it:** open the live page from this repository's GitHub Pages link.

**Read the article:** *Credit, Debit and Prepaid: Whose Money Is It?* on Medium.

Amounts are examples and fees are simplified.
